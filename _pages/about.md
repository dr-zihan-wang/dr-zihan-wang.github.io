<h3><a href="#" style="color: #4a9eda; font-weight: bold; text-decoration: none;">About Me</a></h3>

<p>Welcome! I am Dr Zihan Wang (王梓涵), I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Science Policy Research Unit (SPRU), University of Sussex Business School, and the AHRC Creative Industries Policy and Evidence Centre (PEC). I am also a Visiting Researcher at the Centre for Governance, Regions and Industrial Strategy, University of Bath.</p>

<p>My research examines how innovation unfolds across spatial and organisational contexts. I am particularly interested in how existing skills enable, constrain and redirect future trajectories of innovation, and in how regions and organisations adapt their skill bases in response to technological change, particularly artificial intelligence (AI). More specifically:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Innovation across places</strong> – I examine how skills, as a key regional capability, shape place-based innovation and industrial transformation, and how local labour markets adjust to AI. Drawing on evolutionary economic geography, this stream investigates why some places are better able to develop new economic activities than others, with a particular focus on left-behind places, old industrial regions, and industrial clusters, including creative and manufacturing clusters.</li>
<li><strong>Innovation within organisations</strong> – I examine how AI reshapes skill demand, occupational boundaries and the division of labour within firms, as well as how skills are developed through informal institutional settings such as coworking spaces.</li>
</ul>

<p>A central aim of my research is to bridge academic research and policy practice by generating evidence that informs innovation policy, place-based industrial strategy, skills policy and the future of work.</p>

<p>Methodologically, my research is primarily quantitative, using large-scale datasets including online job postings, LinkedIn employment histories, BACS transactions (bank transfers) and questionnaires. I am also developing mixed-method and qualitative approaches.</p>

<p>Before joining the University of Sussex Business School in 2025, I completed my PhD in Economic Geography at the School of Management, University of Bath (2022–2025). I also hold an MSc in Finance from Fudan University (2018–2020), a BSc in Economics (2014–2018), and a BA in Modern Languages from Wuhan University (2015–2018).</p>

<p>Prior to academia, I gained two years of industry experience as a Senior Consulting Analyst at Accenture (2020–2022).</p>
